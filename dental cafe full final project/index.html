Common questions

Quick answers before you visit.

Is root canal treatment painful?

No. With local anaesthesia the procedure is comfortable, and it relieves the pain caused by infection.

Do you offer braces and clear aligners?

Yes. Dr. Abdullah is an orthodontic specialist (FCPS) and plans both options with you.

How do dental implants work?

An implant replaces the tooth root and supports a crown. Dr. Umair Bhalli specialises in implants and gum health.
